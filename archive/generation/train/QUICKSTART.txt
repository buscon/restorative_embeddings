📞 TROUBLESHOOTING:

    Q: "ModuleNotFoundError: stable_audio_tools"
    A: Re-run 01_setup_environment.py to reinstall dependencies

    Q: "CUDA out of memory"
    A: Reduce batch_size to 1, increase grad_accum_steps to 8-16
       OR use SAO Small instead of Large

    Q: "Training loss doesn't decrease"
    A: Check learning rate (try 1e-5), ensure data loads correctly
       OR check GPU memory usage with nvidia-smi

    Q: "No pleasantness effect in results (r < 0.3)"
    A: Try label variant (b), coarsen bins to 3 words, or add ISD data
